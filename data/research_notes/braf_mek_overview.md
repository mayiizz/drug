# BRAF-targeted therapy in melanoma (curated research note)

## Concept
BRAF V600-mutant melanoma responds to selective BRAF inhibitors.

## Methods
- BRAF V600-selective inhibitors (e.g. vemurafenib)

## Limitations
- Rapid resistance when used alone
- Cutaneous adverse events recorded in trial literature
