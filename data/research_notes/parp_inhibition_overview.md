# PARP inhibition in BRCA-associated breast cancer (curated research note)

## Concept
Synthetic lethality via PARP inhibition in homologous-recombination–deficient tumors.

## Methods
- PARP1-directed inhibitors (e.g. olaparib)

## Limitations
- Hematologic toxicity
- Resistance mechanisms under active study
