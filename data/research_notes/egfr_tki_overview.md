# EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor approaches (curated research note)

Demo research note for CURA's knowledge graph. Not a substitute for primary literature.

## Concept
EGFR inhibition is a central strategy in EGFR-mutant non-small-cell lung cancer.

## Methods discussed
- First-generation EGFR TKIs (e.g. erlotinib, gefitinib)
- Third-generation EGFR TKIs (e.g. osimertinib) for resistant disease

## Recorded limitations (demo graph)
- Skin toxicity / rash
- Diarrhea / GI toxicity
- Acquired resistance (including T790M-era sequencing stories in the literature)
